# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS — requested by the owner for a single-file-family landing they can open and sell tomorrow, without a framework.

## Users

People in Samambaia (DF) who want a nearby gym: residents starting or restarting training, looking for musculação and aulas coletivas, deciding whether to visit or message WhatsApp before committing to a plan.

Secondary audience: the gym owner presenting this landing to a buyer tomorrow as a complete conversion page.

## Product Purpose

A public landing page for Academia Majori Fit Samambaia that answers where it is, when it is open, what happens inside, and gets the visitor to WhatsApp or a visit. Success is a message or a walk-in, not an online checkout.

## Positioning

Neighborhood gym in Samambaia Norte (QS 429), open from 5h on weekdays, with a direct WhatsApp door. It is not a national chain landing and not the Sol Nascente / Ceilândia unit.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Instagram (@majorifitsamambaia), WhatsApp shares, or a buyer demo. They scan on a phone, check hours and location, then tap WhatsApp. Staff close the sale in conversation or at the front desk. No online payment, no member login.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single conversion: open WhatsApp to `(61) 9506-8117` with a prefilled visit/matrícula message.
- Hours (owner-provided art): Monday–Friday 5:00–23:00; Saturday and Sunday 8:00–14:00.
- Address: Quadra QS 429, Conjunto A, Lote 03, Samambaia Norte, Brasília–DF.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/majorifitsamambaia/
- No prices, plans, or promotions on the page until the owner supplies them.
- Do not mix facts from the Sol Nascente / Ceilândia Majori Fit (TotalPass listing, different hours, different phone).
- Modalities of the Samambaia unit are not independently verified. Copy may speak to musculação and a neighborhood training floor; named aulas (fit dance, funcional, pilates, jump, etc.) stay generic or marked as typical of the brand unless confirmed.
- Testimonials on the page are labeled examples for the owner to replace; they are not real Samambaia reviews.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Majori Fit (wordmark in owner art reads MAJORIFIT).
- Logo: black square, orange rounded bar, white bar, white disc — path: owner-supplied PNG.
- Palette pinned by the owner: black, white, and the logo orange (high-chroma vermillion/orange, not terracotta).
- Voice: direct, local, invitation to visit — not chain-hype and not invented guarantees.
- Technology request: HTML, CSS, JS; rich interaction feedback (hover, press, form/WhatsApp confirmation, toast/status), not a static brochure.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo PNG (owner).
- Hours graphic (owner).
- Public registry: ACADEMIA MAJORI FIT II LTDA, QS 429 Conjunto A Lote 03, Samambaia Norte, phone (61) 9506-8117.
- Instagram URL supplied by owner; profile content was not successfully fetched.
- No real Samambaia testimonials, interior photos, class schedule, or price list on hand.
- Public reviews found online belong to the Sol Nascente unit and must not be republished as this gym's proof.

## Product Principles

- Convert on WhatsApp; every path ends in a message or a map pin, not a dead form.
- Stay factually local: Samambaia hours, address, and phone only.
- Label anything authored as example (testimonials, stock atmosphere) so a buyer is not sold a fake review.
- Feedback is part of the product: taps, hover, errors, and success states must answer immediately.
- Do not invent price, results, or celebrity-trainer claims.
